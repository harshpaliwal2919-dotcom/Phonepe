Every bar is one quarter, in billions of payments. The highlighted bars belong to the year you picked.

What stood out

Half the volume, five states

Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh made 52% of all 2025 payments.

The next wave is in the east and north-east

Assam grew 52% in 2025, Arunachal Pradesh 49% and West Bengal 41%, while Karnataka grew 23% and Rajasthan 20%.

Payments got smaller, and more everyday

The average payment fell from ₹1,467 in 2018 to ₹1,209 in 2025. Shop and merchant payments grew from 49% to 62% of volume; person-to-person fell from 47% to 32%.

Same app, very different habits

Telangana averaged about 365 payments per registered user in 2025. Kerala managed 66 and Manipur 46. Users are counted where they registered, so read this as a habit signal, not a precise rate.

Source: PhonePe Pulse open data (github.com/PhonePe/pulse), state-level quarterly files, Q1 2018 to Q2 2026.
